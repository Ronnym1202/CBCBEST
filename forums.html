CBC Discussion Forums

A professional, open space for Kenyan students, teachers, parents, and education stakeholders to discuss the Competency Based Curriculum — pathway choices, classroom strategies, assessment, careers, and policy.

📌
Community Guidelines: These forums are a respectful, constructive space. All discussions must be accurate, helpful, and relevant to CBC Kenya. Spam, personal attacks, and misinformation will be removed. Posts are stored in your browser and shared across sessions on the same device. Be kind — many students, new teachers, and parents are learning alongside you.
