Share a Resource

Share your CBC materials with Kenyan educators. Upload your file to Google Drive first, then paste the shareable link below.

📋 How to get your Google Drive link
  1. Go to drive.google.com and upload your file
  2. Right-click the file → click "Share"
  3. Under "General access" select "Anyone with the link"
  4. Click "Copy link" and paste it below
Must be a Google Drive link set to "Anyone with the link can view"
ℹ️ Teachers: your resource will be published immediately. Students: your upload will be reviewed within 24 hours before going live.
