Senior School Officially Begins: Kenya's First CBC Grade 10 Cohort Enters Pathways

📅 June 2, 2026 · ✍️ Ronny Mwenda · 📖 12 min read

Kenya's education system has entered a landmark phase. Beginning in the 2025–2026 academic year, the first cohort of learners under the Competency Based Curriculum has entered Senior Secondary School (Grades 10–12). These are students who began PP1 when CBC was piloted in 2017 and have progressed through the full 2-6-3 ladder. They now choose between three distinct pathways: STEM, Social Sciences, or Arts & Sports Science.

This transition marks the moment the CBC's pathway model becomes real for Kenyan families. The Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA), sat at the end of Grade 9, informs — but does not dictate — pathway placement. Aptitude, interest, and school counsellor guidance all play a role. As a Mathematics and Computer Science teacher in Embu, I have seen firsthand how critical it is for learners and parents to understand each pathway's demands and opportunities before making this choice.
