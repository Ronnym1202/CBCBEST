About these practice materials

These are original, KICD-aligned practice questions written by Ronny Mwenda, based on published CBC Curriculum Designs, KNEC assessment frameworks, and MoE Kenya guidelines (BECF, KICD subject designs, 2023–2025). They reflect the format, competency focus, strand coverage, and difficulty level of actual KPSEA and KJSEA assessments. The first KJSEA cohort sits in 2026/2027 — effective preparation starts now. KPSEA

Grade 6 Exit Assessment

Kenya Primary School Education Assessment

Administered at the end of Grade 6 by KNEC under KICD and MoE direction. KPSEA is a competency-based profiling assessment, not a ranking examination. Results are expressed in four performance bands and used for Junior Secondary School placement.

Multiple sittings · ~2 hrs each
Grading

KJSEA

Grade 9 Exit Assessment

Kenya Junior School Education Assessment

Administered at the end of Grade 9. KJSEA uses a portfolio-plus-examination model: learners submit a Junior Secondary School Portfolio (JSSP) alongside KNEC-administered written and practical components. Results guide Senior School pathway placement.

CBC Performance Bands — How Results Are Reported

CBC assessments do not produce aggregate scores. Learner achievement is reported in four performance bands describing what a learner can do. Each subject has its own KICD-published band descriptors.

EE
Exceeds Expectation
Consistently demonstrates competencies beyond grade-level expectations with depth and precision.
ME
Meets Expectation
Demonstrates all expected competencies at the required grade level, accurately and consistently.
AE
Approaches Expectation
Demonstrates most competencies but requires some support to achieve full grade-level mastery.
BE
Below Expectation
Requires significant additional support and intervention across most competency areas.
