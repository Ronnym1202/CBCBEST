// 01 · Junior Secondary School

Junior Secondary Learning Areas (Grades 7–9)

Junior Secondary School (JSS) is the foundational phase of senior learning. All learners — regardless of future pathway — study 12 compulsory learning areas. These are broad, integrated, and designed to develop the whole learner before pathway selection at Grade 9. The Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA) at the end of Grade 9 informs Senior School pathway placement.

📋

KICD Note on JSS Learning Areas

All 12 JSS learning areas are compulsory. There are no electives at this stage. Learners are assessed continuously through formative tools and a summative KJSEA at Grade 9. Results guide — but do not restrict — pathway choice. Source: KICD Basic Education Curriculum Framework (BECF).

// 02 · Senior School (Grades 10–12)

Senior School Pathway Subjects

From Grade 10, learners specialise in one of three pathways: STEM, Social Sciences, or Arts & Sports Science. Each pathway has compulsory core subjects and optional electives. All learners also continue English, Kiswahili, and community service learning regardless of pathway. The Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) is sat at the end of Grade 12, with 60% coursework and 40% external examination.

ℹ️

How Subject Selection Works at Grade 10

Each pathway has a set of core (compulsory) subjects all pathway learners must take, plus elective subjects chosen from within the pathway (typically 2–3 electives). Schools must offer at minimum the core subjects; electives depend on school capacity and teacher availability. See full pathway guide →

// Senior School Pathway 01

STEM Pathway

Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics. Prepares learners for careers in scientific research, technology, medicine, engineering, and data-driven industries. Emphasises inquiry-based learning, laboratory practice, computational thinking, and mathematical modelling.

CORE Compulsory for all STEM learners — Grades 10, 11 & 12

Mathematics

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

The backbone of the STEM pathway. Develops advanced quantitative reasoning, problem-solving, and modelling skills essential across all STEM disciplines. All learners in the STEM pathway must take Mathematics.

Key Strands / Content Areas
  • Numbers & Algebra: Sequences, polynomials, surds, logarithms, indices, quadratic & simultaneous equations
  • Calculus: Differentiation, integration, rates of change, maxima & minima, applications
  • Statistics & Probability: Data collection & analysis, measures of central tendency & dispersion, frequency distributions, probability models
  • Geometry & Trigonometry: Coordinate geometry, circles, transformation, trigonometric ratios, sine & cosine rules
  • Financial Mathematics: Compound interest, depreciation, loan repayments, investment calculations
  • Vectors & Matrices: Operations, determinants, applications in geometry and transformations
Abstract reasoningModellingData interpretationProof writingProblem decomposition
📌 Sample project: Model population growth in a Kenyan county using exponential functions and present a 10-year projection with statistical analysis.

Assessment: Written tests, problem-solving portfolios, mathematical investigations, CATs each term, and KCSE written paper (Paper 1 & 2).

Biology

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Explores living systems at the cellular, organismal, and ecosystem level. Develops scientific inquiry, laboratory techniques, and understanding of life processes relevant to medicine, agriculture, and environmental management.

Key Strands / Content Areas
  • Cell Biology: Cell structure, cell division (mitosis & meiosis), cell transport mechanisms
  • Genetics & Heredity: DNA structure, Mendelian genetics, mutation, genetic engineering, biotechnology
  • Human Physiology: Nutrition, transport, respiration, excretion, reproduction, nervous & endocrine systems
  • Ecology & Environment: Food webs, nutrient cycles, population dynamics, conservation, climate change
  • Microbiology & Immunology: Microorganisms, disease causation, vaccines, antibiotics
  • Plant Biology: Photosynthesis, plant transport, growth hormones, reproduction
Lab techniquesScientific methodData recordingMicroscopyDissection
📌 Sample project: Investigate the effect of different concentrations of salt solution on plant cells (osmosis) and present findings using graphs and a written scientific report.

Assessment: Practical lab reports, observation schedules, written tests, research projects, KCSE Paper 1 (theory) & Paper 2 (practical/essay).

Chemistry

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops deep understanding of matter, its properties, reactions, and applications. Practical chemistry is central — learners spend significant time in labs performing titrations, preparations, and qualitative analysis.

Key Strands / Content Areas
  • Atomic Structure & Periodicity: Electronic configuration, periodic trends, chemical bonding
  • Stoichiometry: Mole concept, balancing equations, yield calculations, empirical formulae
  • Organic Chemistry: Hydrocarbons, functional groups, reactions, polymers, petrochemicals
  • Thermochemistry: Enthalpy changes, Hess's Law, bond energies, energy profiles
  • Electrochemistry: Electrolysis, galvanic cells, corrosion, applications
  • Industrial Chemistry: Haber process, Contact process, water treatment, polymer manufacture
TitrationChemical safetyQualitative analysisReport writing
📌 Sample project: Test and compare the hardness of water from three local sources using soap test and EDTA titration, then propose cost-effective treatment options.

Assessment: Practical lab reports, written tests, CATs, KCSE Paper 1 (theory) & Paper 2 (practical).

Physics

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Explores the fundamental laws governing matter and energy. Develops engineering intuition, measurement precision, and the analytical skills underpinning all physical technology — from electronics to aviation and renewable energy.

Key Strands / Content Areas
  • Mechanics: Motion, Newton's Laws, momentum, energy, circular motion, simple machines
  • Electricity & Magnetism: Current, resistance, circuits, electromagnetic induction, AC/DC, motors & generators
  • Waves & Optics: Wave properties, reflection, refraction, diffraction, lenses, mirrors, sound, light
  • Thermodynamics: Heat transfer, thermal expansion, gas laws, kinetic theory, refrigeration
  • Modern Physics: Radioactivity, nuclear reactions, photoelectric effect, X-rays, medical applications
  • Electronics: Semiconductors, diodes, transistors, logic gates, cathode ray oscilloscope
Circuit buildingMeasurement precisionGraphical analysisError analysis
📌 Sample project: Construct a simple DC electric motor from recycled materials, measure its RPM and voltage output, and relate findings to electromagnetic induction principles.

Assessment: Practical experiments, written tests, CATs, KCSE Paper 1 & Paper 2 (practical).

Computer Science

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Prepares learners for Kenya's digital economy. Covers programming, data structures, networks, databases, cybersecurity, and emerging technologies. Strongly aligned with Kenya's digital transformation agenda and ICT Authority goals.

Key Strands / Content Areas
  • Programming & Algorithms: Python/JavaScript syntax, control structures, functions, recursion, sorting & searching algorithms
  • Data & Databases: Data types, SQL basics, database design, spreadsheet models, data validation
  • Computer Systems: Hardware components, operating systems, memory management, binary & data representation
  • Networks & Internet: Network topologies, protocols (TCP/IP, HTTP), cloud computing, IoT concepts
  • Cybersecurity & Ethics: Digital security, privacy, encryption basics, responsible use, cybercrime laws in Kenya
  • Emerging Technologies: Artificial Intelligence basics, machine learning concepts, robotics, mobile development
PythonComputational thinkingDebuggingDatabase designWeb fundamentals
📌 Sample project: Design and deploy a simple school information system using a database, a query interface, and a web-based front-end — documenting the development process.

Assessment: Programming projects, portfolio of coded solutions, written tests, practical exam, KCSE written & practical papers.

ELECTIVE Choose from — subject to school availability

Agriculture & Land Use

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Applies scientific knowledge to farming systems, land management, food security, and agribusiness. Especially relevant for Kenya's agricultural economy.

  • Crop Production: Soil science, planting systems, pest management, irrigation, post-harvest handling
  • Animal Production: Livestock breeds, feeds, health management, dairy & poultry production
  • Agribusiness: Farm records, value chain analysis, agricultural marketing, financial planning
  • Soil & Water Management: Soil testing, conservation, water harvesting, hydroponics
📌 Sample project: Set up a drip irrigation vegetable plot, monitor growth under different watering regimes, and present a cost-benefit analysis of the harvest.

Technical Drawing & Design

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops spatial reasoning, engineering drawing skills, and design thinking for learners targeting architecture, civil engineering, or manufacturing careers.

  • Orthographic, isometric, oblique and perspective drawing
  • Dimensioning, tolerances, and sectional views
  • Geometric constructions and plane figures
  • Introduction to CAD (Computer-Aided Design) tools
📌 Sample project: Produce a technical drawing portfolio for a proposed school building addition, including floor plan, elevations, and sections.

Home Science (Science Application)

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Applies biology, chemistry, and nutrition science to food technology, textile science, and household management, with strong links to hospitality, dietetics, and consumer science careers.

  • Food science: nutrients, food processing, preservation, safety & labelling
  • Textile science: fibres, weaves, care & laundering, fashion design basics
  • Consumer education: budgeting, household management, environmental sustainability
  • Child development & family health
📌 Sample project: Develop a nutrition intervention plan for a school canteen, including food testing, label analysis, and cost-per-meal budgeting.

// Senior School Pathway 02

Social Sciences Pathway

Humanities, social studies, and civic education. Cultivates responsible citizens, community leaders, and critical thinkers who understand human systems, policy, governance, and global connections. Suited for careers in law, public administration, education, journalism, NGO leadership, and diplomacy.

CORE Compulsory for all Social Sciences learners

History & Government

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops historical consciousness, civic awareness, and an understanding of how governments and societies have evolved. Encourages critical reading of sources, debate, and active citizenship.

  • Kenyan History: Pre-colonial societies, colonialism, independence movements, post-independence development, devolution
  • African History: Pre-colonial civilisations, Trans-Atlantic slave trade, colonisation of Africa, liberation movements, OAU/AU
  • World History: World Wars, Cold War, United Nations, globalisation, international relations
  • Government & Civics: Kenya's Constitution 2010, governance structures, human rights, democratic participation, devolved government
  • Source Analysis: Primary & secondary sources, bias detection, oral history, document analysis
Source analysisCritical thinkingEssay writingDebateCivic engagement
📌 Sample project: Conduct oral history interviews with three community elders about the impact of colonial land policies, then compile a written report connecting findings to post-independence land reform.

Assessment: Essays, source-based questions, oral presentations, research projects, portfolio of community engagement, KCSE written papers.

Geography

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Analyses the physical and human world — landforms, climate, population, settlement, and resource management. Fieldwork and GIS tools bring geography to life in real Kenyan environments.

  • Physical Geography: Plate tectonics, weathering & erosion, drainage systems, climatic zones, soils
  • Human Geography: Population distribution, migration, urbanisation, settlement patterns, economic activities
  • Environmental Geography: Deforestation, desertification, water scarcity, climate change impacts in East Africa
  • Map Work & Fieldwork: Topographic maps, aerial photos, GIS basics, transect surveys, field sketches
  • Kenya's Physical & Economic Geography: Landforms, rivers, lakes, highlands, agriculture zones, tourism
Map readingFieldworkGIS basicsData analysisReport writing
📌 Sample project: Carry out a transect fieldwork study along a local river, identify signs of erosion and deposition, and propose community conservation strategies backed by a written report and annotated maps.

Religious Education (CRE / IRE / HRE)

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops ethical reasoning, moral values, and respect for religious and cultural diversity. Learners select one of CRE (Christian), IRE (Islamic), or HRE (Hindu) based on faith background.

  • CRE: Old & New Testament study, Christian ethics, the church in Kenya, contemporary moral issues
  • IRE: Quranic studies, Hadith, Islamic jurisprudence, moral conduct, Islamic civilisation in East Africa
  • HRE: Hindu scripture, dharma, ethics, rituals, Hindu contributions to Kenyan culture
  • Cross-Faith Themes: Interfaith dialogue, conflict resolution, environmental stewardship, social justice
📌 Sample project: Organise an interfaith community service event and document how different faith traditions motivate participants toward a shared social good.

Business Studies

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops entrepreneurial thinking, financial literacy, marketing skills, and understanding of how businesses operate in Kenya's economy. Directly applicable to SME development and formal commerce careers.

  • Entrepreneurship: Business planning, innovation, start-up financing, social enterprise
  • Accounting: Double-entry bookkeeping, trial balance, income statements, balance sheets, cash flow
  • Marketing: Market research, consumer behaviour, pricing, promotion, distribution channels
  • Business Environment: Types of business organisations, business ethics, labour relations, Kenya's taxation system
  • ICT in Business: Spreadsheet modelling, e-commerce, digital marketing, mobile money systems
AccountingBusiness planningFinancial literacyMarket research
📌 Sample project: Develop a full business plan for a community-based enterprise, including market analysis, startup costs, projected income statement, and a pitch presentation.

Economics

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Introduces the study of resource allocation, market systems, and economic policy — with a strong focus on Kenya and the East African Community (EAC).

  • Demand and supply theory; market equilibrium; price mechanism
  • National income accounting; GDP, GNP, and standards of living
  • Money, banking, and fiscal/monetary policy in Kenya
  • International trade, balance of payments, exchange rates, EAC integration
  • Development economics; poverty, inequality, Vision 2030
📌 Sample project: Track the price of five basic commodities in a local market over eight weeks and analyse the data against national inflation data from the KNBS.

Sociology & Social Work

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Examines social structures, institutions, and challenges — including family, gender, health systems, and community welfare. Prepares learners for social work, counselling, public health, and community development careers.

  • Sociological theories; culture, norms, values, socialisation
  • Family structures in Kenya; marriage systems, parenting roles, family policy
  • Social issues: gender inequality, child protection, GBV, disability rights
  • Community development and social welfare systems in Kenya
  • Research methods in social sciences; surveys, interviews, observation
📌 Sample project: Conduct a community needs assessment using interviews and questionnaires, then present a social intervention proposal to a panel.

// Senior School Pathway 03

Arts & Sports Science Pathway

Creative arts, performance, and sports science. Nurtures talent in music, drama, visual arts, athletics, and media. Aligned to Kenya's creative economy, sports industry, and the growing demand for skilled professionals in film, design, coaching, and sports medicine.

CORE Compulsory for all Arts & Sports learners

Music

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops musical literacy, performance skills, and cultural appreciation across Kenyan, African, and world music traditions. Learners engage in both theoretical study and practical performance.

  • Music Theory: Notation, scales, intervals, harmony, rhythm, time signatures, chord progressions
  • Music History: African traditional music, Western classical eras, development of popular and contemporary Kenyan music
  • Performance: Vocal technique, instrumental skills (voice, keyboard, guitar, percussion, traditional instruments), ensemble playing
  • Composition & Arrangement: Writing melodies, harmonising, adapting traditional songs, basic digital audio workstation (DAW) use
  • Music Technology: Recording, sound engineering basics, music production
Sight-readingPerformanceCompositionEar trainingEnsemble
📌 Sample project: Compose and record an original piece inspired by a traditional Kenyan musical form, then perform it live for a school audience and write a composer's note.

Visual Arts & Design

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Develops artistic expression, design skills, and visual communication across traditional and digital media. Prepares learners for careers in fine art, graphic design, fashion, architecture, and the creative economy.

  • Drawing & Painting: Life drawing, perspective, colour theory, watercolour, oil and acrylic media
  • Graphic Design: Typography, layout, branding, poster design, digital tools (Canva, Inkscape, Photoshop basics)
  • Sculpture & 3D Design: Clay modelling, assemblage, installation, product design concepts
  • Textile & Fashion Design: Fabric properties, pattern drafting, garment construction, Kenyan textile traditions
  • Art History & Criticism: African art traditions, contemporary Kenyan artists, critique methodology, visiting exhibitions
Colour theoryPortfolio buildingCritiqueDigital art toolsVisual storytelling
📌 Sample project: Design and produce a graphic identity (logo, poster, social media template) for a real community organisation, presenting the design rationale and iterative process.

Physical Education & Sports Science

Core
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Combines practical athletic training with the scientific study of human movement, exercise physiology, and sports management. Prepares learners for coaching, sports administration, physical therapy, and professional sport.

  • Exercise Physiology: Musculoskeletal system, cardiovascular response, energy systems, VO₂ max, recovery
  • Sports Coaching & Officiating: Rules of selected sports, coaching pedagogy, referee certification pathways, game analysis
  • Sports Psychology: Motivation, goal setting, concentration, anxiety management, team dynamics
  • Sports Nutrition: Macronutrients, hydration, meal timing, weight management, supplements ethics
  • Sports Management: Event organisation, sports administration, sponsorship, media relations, facility management
  • Injury Prevention & First Aid: Common sports injuries, RICE method, taping, safe training practices
CoachingSports analysisEvent planningSports first aidLeadership
📌 Sample project: Plan, organise, and referee a school sports tournament; prepare a report including scheduling, budget, results analysis, and a reflection on officiating challenges.

Performing Arts (Dance, Drama & Theatre)

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Combines movement, storytelling, and theatrical production. Learners develop stage presence, creative direction, and the ability to communicate complex ideas through performance.

  • Stage acting: character development, improvisation, script analysis, physical theatre
  • Dance: Kenyan traditional dances, contemporary dance, choreography, notation
  • Theatre production: directing, set design, lighting, costume, sound, stage management
  • Playwriting: dramatic structure, dialogue, theme, audience awareness
  • Festival & competition theatre: Kenya Schools Drama Festival standards
📌 Sample project: Write, direct, and stage a 15-minute original play addressing a current social issue, including a post-show panel discussion with the audience.

Film, Television & Animation

Elective
Gr 10Gr 11Gr 12

Prepares learners for Kenya's growing film and media industry. Covers production, post-production, and digital storytelling across formats. Aligned to Kenya's ICT and creative economy policy goals.

  • Pre-production: concept development, scriptwriting, storyboarding, production planning
  • Camera & lighting: shot composition, exposure, cinematography techniques
  • Direction & acting for camera
  • Post-production: video editing (DaVinci Resolve / CapCut), colour grading, sound mixing
  • Animation fundamentals: 2D, stop-motion, digital animation principles
  • Broadcasting & media ethics; copyright, responsible content creation
📌 Sample project: Produce a 3–5 minute short documentary about a local community issue from concept through final edit, including all production and post-production stages.
// 03 · Cross-Cutting Competencies

Competencies Assessed Across All Subjects

The seven CBC core competencies are not subject-specific — they cut across all learning areas and pathways. Every subject teacher is responsible for deliberately developing and assessing these competencies through daily learning activities, projects, and community engagement tasks.

Competency How it appears in subjects Typical assessment method
Communication & Collaboration Group projects, oral presentations, peer reviews, debate, science lab reports written collaboratively Observation schedule, peer assessment, oral Q&A, rubric
Critical Thinking & Problem Solving Mathematical modelling, source analysis in History, case studies in Business, chemistry experiments Written tests, project assessment, anecdotal records, rubric
Creativity & Imagination Art portfolios, technology design, music composition, drama writing, innovative science solutions Portfolio, project, rating scale, exhibition
Citizenship Community Service Learning (CSL) projects, History debates, RE ethics, Geography conservation Community service log, reflection journals, interviews, presentations
Digital Literacy Computer Science, digital art tools, business spreadsheets, research using ICT, film production Practical exam, project, portfolio of digital work
Learning to Learn Self-assessment tasks, portfolio reflection, independent research, study skills across all subjects Self-assessment, portfolio, learning logs, checklists
Self-Efficacy Goal-setting in sports, confidence in performance arts, perseverance in STEM problem-solving Self-assessment, anecdotal records, counsellor/teacher observation
✅

Common & Core Subjects Across All Pathways

Regardless of Senior School pathway, all learners must study English Language and Kiswahili throughout Grades 10–12. Community Service Learning (CSL) is also compulsory across all pathways and must be documented in each learner's portfolio. These are examined in the KCSE alongside pathway subjects.

// 04 · For Teachers

Delivering CBC Subjects Effectively

Each subject in CBC demands a specific pedagogical approach. Below are the key requirements and responsibilities every CBC teacher must fulfil when delivering any learning area — as per KICD curriculum designs and TSC teacher standards.

📋

Scheme of Work & Lesson Plans

Teachers must prepare termly schemes of work and individual lesson plans for every topic, specifying the learning outcome, teaching/learning activities, resources, and assessment method. Lesson plans in CBC follow a learner-centred format — not lecture-style delivery.

📊

Formative Assessment Records

All 13 KICD assessment tools must be applied appropriately throughout the term. Teachers maintain individual learner records, track progress against strand learning outcomes, and give timely, specific feedback. Records feed into the continuous assessment score (60% weighting).

🏗️

Project-Based Learning (PBL)

Each learning area must include project work that connects subject content to real-world contexts. Projects should be authentic, community-relevant, and allow learners to apply multiple competencies. Teachers facilitate rather than direct, allowing learner agency.

🧩

Differentiated Instruction

CBC requires teachers to address diverse learning needs within the same classroom. Content, process, and product must be adapted for learners with different abilities, learning styles, and needs — including learners with special needs, gifted learners, and those requiring remediation.

💻

ICT Integration

All teachers must integrate ICT into their teaching — using digital resources, online research, subject-specific software, and eLearning platforms where available. This is especially required for STEM and Computer Science teachers but applies across all pathways.

📁

Learner Portfolio Management

Teachers guide learners in building subject portfolios that document progress, projects, and reflections. Portfolio assessment is one of the 13 KICD tools and contributes to the continuous assessment score. Portfolios are reviewed by learners, teachers, and external assessors.
