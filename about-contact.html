// 02 · About CBC Best

What CBC Best Is — and Why It Exists

CBC Best was built to solve a real problem: Kenya's transformative new curriculum was being misunderstood at every level — by learners who didn't know which pathway to choose, by teachers unsure of qualification requirements, and by parents confused about what CBC actually demands of their children.

🎯

The Problem CBC Best Solves

Since CBC's rollout, reliable, structured, KICD-aligned information has been scattered across government documents, social media rumours, and unofficial sources. CBC Best consolidates everything in one place — accurately, accessibly, and with depth.

  • Pathway confusion — which stream is right for each learner?
  • Teacher qualification gaps — what does TSC actually require?
  • Assessment misunderstanding — how do CATs and portfolios work?
  • Career disconnection — where does each pathway actually lead?
  • Parent anxiety — how does 2-6-3-3 differ from 8-4-4?
📘

What CBC Best Provides

Every section of this site is written from a teacher's perspective — grounded in classroom reality, aligned to KICD curriculum designs, and cross-referenced with TSC and KNEC guidelines.

  • Complete pathway breakdowns — STEM, Social Sciences, Arts & Sports
  • KICD-aligned subject strands and learning outcomes
  • All 13 KICD assessment tools explained with subject examples
  • TSC teacher qualification requirements per pathway
  • Dedicated Mathematics & Computer Science STEM hub
  • Discussion forums for students and teachers
  • CBC news, FAQ, and downloadable resources
✅

Content Standards & Integrity

All content on CBC Best is original, researched, and written by Ronny Mwenda. No content is copied from external sources. All factual claims are sourced from KICD curriculum designs, TSC guidelines, KNEC frameworks, and relevant Kenyan legislation.

  • No content is fabricated or misleading
  • Curriculum information is regularly reviewed against KICD updates
  • Teacher requirements reflect current TSC deployment standards
  • Original writing throughout — AdSense and Search Console compliant
  • No paid promotions or sponsored content influence what is written
👥

Who CBC Best Serves

The site is deliberately designed for multiple audiences — each with distinct information needs around Kenya's CBC.

  • Students (Grades 7–12): pathway selection, subject guides, career connections
  • Teachers: qualification requirements, assessment tools, lesson planning frameworks
  • Parents & guardians: understanding the structure, expectations, and transition points
  • School administrators: policy context, staffing standards, KICD/TSC alignment
  • Government & researchers: accurate curriculum data and implementation context
