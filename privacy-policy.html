Overview & Scope of This Policy

Who we are and what this policy covers

CBC Best (operating at https://cbcbest.netlify.app) is an independent educational platform created and maintained by Ronny Mwenda (Ronny Best), a Mathematics and Computer Science teacher based in Embu County, Kenya. The platform is part of a broader digital ecosystem that also includes Bestronny.netlify.app (Ronny Best Mathematics resources), RonnyMwenda.netlify.app (personal portfolio and biography), and RonnyGame.netlify.app (educational games).

This Privacy Policy applies to all pages, features, discussion forums, downloadable resources, and contact mechanisms on cbcbest.netlify.app. It does not govern the practices of third-party websites that we link to, including KICD, TSC, KNEC, or the Ministry of Education.

CBC Best publishes original educational content aligned to Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) as specified in the Basic Education Curriculum Framework (BECF) produced by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD), and operates with awareness of teacher professional standards set by the Teachers Service Commission (TSC). No content on this site is intended to misrepresent, duplicate, or replace official KICD or TSC publications.

Our commitment: CBC Best is a genuinely independent educational resource. We do not sell personal data, share it with data brokers, or use it for purposes unrelated to improving your experience on this platform. Data protection is not an afterthought — it is built into how we operate.

Information We Collect

What data is collected, and how

CBC Best collects two categories of information: data you voluntarily provide to us, and data automatically generated by your device when you visit the site.

📝 Voluntarily Provided Data

  • Forum participation — Display name, publicly visible posts and replies
  • Contact form submissions — Name, email address, and message content
  • Newsletter subscription — Email address only (opt-in basis)
  • Resource downloads — No personal information required for downloads

📊 Automatically Collected Data

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URL
  • Usage data — Pages visited, time on page, navigation patterns
  • Device data — Screen resolution, language setting, device category
  • Local storage — Forum preferences, selected tabs (stored in your own browser)

What we do NOT collect

  • National ID numbers or passport numbers — never requested or stored
  • Financial or payment information — CBC Best is a free platform; no transactions occur
  • Precise geolocation data — we do not use GPS-level location tracking
  • Biometric data — no fingerprints, face recognition, or voice data
  • Sensitive personal data — health status, political opinions, religion, ethnicity are not collected
  • Passwords — the site uses browser-based localStorage and does not operate its own authentication system
📢 Public forum reminder: Forum posts are visible to all site visitors. Do not share personal identifying details — such as your phone number, home address, national ID, school name, or exam results — in any public forum thread.

04

Cookies & Local Storage

How we store data in your browser

CBC Best primarily uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage rather than traditional HTTP cookies. These are technologies that store small amounts of data in your own browser — not on our servers.

What we store in your browser

  • Forum post data — Public posts you make in the forums are saved in localStorage so they persist across sessions
  • Navigation preferences — Your last active forum tab or section selection
  • Contact form drafts — Temporary storage of form inputs while you are typing
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) cache — Static assets (HTML, CSS, images) cached via the service worker for offline access

What we do NOT store

  • Third-party advertising cookies (unless Google AdSense is active — see Section 10)
  • Cross-site tracking pixels or social media tracking scripts
  • Session tokens linked to a centralised account system
Managing browser storage: You may clear all locally stored data at any time through your browser settings — navigate to Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Browsing Data → Cached images and files / Site data. This removes saved forum posts and preferences but does not affect the website's core functionality.

We intend to implement a cookie consent notice compliant with ODPC guidance before activating any third-party advertising. Until then, no persistent advertising cookies are set by CBC Best.

Data Security

How we protect your information

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold, consistent with the requirements of Section 41 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

Technical safeguards

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption — All traffic between your browser and cbcbest.netlify.app is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher, enforced by Netlify's infrastructure
  • Service Worker isolation — The PWA service worker caches only public assets and does not intercept authenticated sessions
  • No centralised user database — Forum data is stored in your browser's localStorage rather than on a remote server, substantially reducing server-side data exposure risk
  • Minimal data collection — We do not collect data beyond what is necessary for the specific function being used (data minimisation principle)

Organisational safeguards

  • Single administrator — Ronny Mwenda is the sole person with access to site configuration, contact form submissions, and newsletter subscriber lists
  • Regular dependency updates — Third-party scripts and hosting configurations are reviewed and updated regularly
  • No third-party data processors — We do not contract external companies to process user data on our behalf (except Netlify for hosting)
Important limitation: No method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we implement best-practice safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and the ODPC in accordance with the Data Protection (General) Regulations 2021.

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and GDPR

Part IV of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 establishes the rights of data subjects. These rights are summarised below. EU residents may additionally rely on equivalent rights under the GDPR.

👁️

Right to Access

Request a copy of personal data we hold about you (Section 26, DPA 2019)

✏️

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data (Section 27, DPA 2019)

🗑️

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") (Section 28, DPA 2019)

🚫

Right to Object

Object to processing of your data for specific purposes (Section 32, DPA 2019)

📦

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Section 29, DPA 2019)

⏸️

Right to Restrict Processing

Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances (Section 30, DPA 2019)

↩️

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw previously given consent at any time without penalty

📋

Right to Lodge a Complaint

Complain directly to the ODPC at odpc.go.ke

To exercise any of these rights, submit a written request via our contact form using the subject line "Privacy Request — [Right Type]" (e.g., "Privacy Request — Erasure"). We will respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request, consistent with the DPA 2019 timelines. No fee is charged for exercising your rights.

Children's Privacy

Special protections for learners under 18

CBC Best serves students across the entire CBC grade structure — including learners in Primary School (Grades 1–6, ages approximately 6–12), Junior Secondary (Grades 7–9, ages approximately 13–15), and Senior Secondary (Grades 10–12, ages approximately 16–18). We take the protection of younger users with the utmost seriousness.

Children under 13 years of age

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent, consistent with Section 51 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and the protections applicable under international frameworks. The discussion forums are designed for educational use and moderated to ensure they remain appropriate for all age groups.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child under the age of 13 has submitted personal information to CBC Best without your consent, please contact us immediately at the email address below. We will promptly investigate and delete the information from our records.

Learners aged 13–18

Learners in this age group may use the site independently for educational purposes. We encourage parents and teachers to review this policy together with their students. Content published on CBC Best is written for academic and educational purposes and aligns with the values and learning outcomes described in the KICD Basic Education Curriculum Framework (BECF).

For teachers and parents: CBC Best is an original, independent educational resource, not affiliated with any school or government body. It does not require student registration to access curriculum content, pathways information, or downloadable resources. Sensitive student data is never a requirement for using this platform.

Advertising Policy

Current and future use of advertising on CBC Best

CBC Best is currently a free, non-commercial educational platform. No advertising is displayed at this time. In the future, we may integrate Google AdSense to generate revenue that supports the cost of maintaining and expanding the platform. If and when this occurs, the following will apply:

Google AdSense — how it works

  • Interest-based advertising: Google uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads that may be relevant to your interests based on your browsing history across Google's network
  • Personally identifiable information: CBC Best will not pass personally identifiable information to Google AdSense. Ads are served based on contextual and interest signals managed entirely by Google
  • Google's role as data processor: Google acts as an independent data controller for its advertising systems. Their data practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps
  • DoubleClick DART cookie: When AdSense is active, Google's DoubleClick DART cookie may be used to serve ads. Users may opt out of this cookie by visiting Google Ads Settings

Our advertising commitments

  • We will never display ads in a way that is misleading, harmful, or inappropriate for a student or teacher audience
  • A cookie consent notice will be displayed before any advertising cookies are set, as required by ODPC guidance
  • We will not show ads on pages or sections specifically designed for young learners under 13
  • All advertising will comply with Google AdSense Programme Policies and Kenya's Consumer Protection Act 2012
No ads currently active. This section is published now in the spirit of transparency and to ensure full compliance with AdSense Publisher Policies, which require a clearly accessible privacy policy that discloses advertising practices before activation.

International Data Transfers

Where your data may be processed

CBC Best is hosted on Netlify's global CDN, which may process data on servers located in the United States, Europe, and other regions. Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, international transfers of personal data are permitted where the recipient country provides an adequate level of protection, or where appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are in place.

Netlify operates under its own Privacy Policy and GDPR/CCPA compliance framework, which we consider provides adequate safeguards for the limited infrastructure data processed through their platform.

Google Fonts requests are routed through Google's CDN and may involve data processing in the United States. Google operates under Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers.

By using CBC Best, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed outside Kenya. We take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers are compliant with applicable law.
